Independent music submissions

Submit Your Music

Send your finished song to Look Who It Is Radio for airplay consideration. We review independent rock, country, Americana, roots, Red Dirt, alternative, and singer-songwriter music for station fit, quality, and replay value.

  • Submit a finished song that is ready for public streaming.
  • Include a downloadable digital audio file or file link.
  • Share your artist information, release details, and the story behind the song.
  • AI-generated music is not accepted.

Submission does not guarantee airplay. Every song is reviewed for station fit, quality, and replay value.
